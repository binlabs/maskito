DateKit Segment Navigation — Single-Input PoC

Gate A experiment: can a single <input> deliver acceptable per-segment screen-reader semantics? Turn on VoiceOver (Cmd+F5), focus the field, and try the keys below. Record what VoiceOver announces on segment move and on step.

  • Tab / Shift+Tab — move between segments
  • ← / → — move between segments
  • ↑ / ↓ — step the active segment
  • Type digits to fill the active segment
